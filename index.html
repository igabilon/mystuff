Iñigo Gabilondo, MD, PhD

Neurologist | Ikerbasque Associate Professor | Visual Biomarkers and Neurodegeneration

Current Positions

Neurologist at the Neurology Service, University Hospital of Cruces. Ikerbasque Associate Professor at Biobizkaia Health Research Institute. Coordinator of the Ad Hoc Committee on New Technologies, Spanish Society of Neurology.

Research Interests

My research focuses on visual biomarkers, retinal imaging, eye-tracking, artificial intelligence, and neurodegenerative diseases, particularly multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s disease, and Alzheimer’s disease.

Academic Background

MD, University of Navarra, 2005. Neurology specialization through the MIR programme at University Hospital of Cruces, 2010. PhD in Medicine, University of Barcelona, Cum Laude, with Extraordinary Doctorate Award.

Selected Metrics

129 Web of Science indexed publications. H-index 27. More than 5,200 citations. Principal investigator of competitive research projects with cumulative funding exceeding €860,000.

BegiBrainLab

Principal investigator of BegiBrainLab, a research group focused on visual pathway biomarkers, OCT, eye-tracking, psychophysics, and AI-based approaches for neurological diseases.
